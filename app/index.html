        Missable trophies

        Built from the official descriptions and the launch guides: only one trophy in the list can be missed. Everything else stays available after the credits, including every side-story finale. The safety net is the same in both cases — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, because the game has no mission replay.

        Story trophies are unmissable: they pop as you play the missions. Trophies marked potentially missable above are the only ones that need a save. The time-sensitive Race Against Yago (Chapter 3, hourglass icon) is not a missable trophy by itself, but it feeds Strangers & Freaks — save before it and race the moment it appears.

        Before you start

        Five things to set up before Gone Fishing. Get these right and the whole list is one clean playthrough.

        1. Turn cheats off and leave them off. Cheats switch trophies and saving off on that save — a cheated session earns nothing.
        2. Save discipline. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave and no mission replay. Keep one save at the start of every chapter, one untouched save from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest.
        3. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning and follow her to the balcony of Jason's house. It is the one thing in the game that can be missed — see Positive Mental Attitude.
        4. Race Yago the moment the hourglass appears (Chapter 3). Save first, then race. It is the only time-sensitive strand in the list.
        5. Play the story straight through first. All 12 chapter and named-mission trophies pop as you go, and the side stories, collectibles and grinds all stay open after the credits.
